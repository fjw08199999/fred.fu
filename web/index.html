About the programme

The B.A. in Foreign Languages and Literatures gives you a deep command of the English language and its literatures, from Chaucer to contemporary global fiction. You will learn to read critically, write persuasively, and think independently — skills valued in every profession.

Literature

Study poetry, drama, and the novel across periods, cultures, and continents.

Language & Linguistics

Explore how English works — its grammar, history, and role in the world today.

Creative Writing

Develop your own voice in fiction, poetry, and non-fiction workshops.

Curriculum

A four-year path from language foundations to literary and linguistic specialisation, built around the department's required and elective courses.

Year 1 — Foundations

  • Freshman English
  • English Oral Training (I)
  • Introduction to Western Literature
  • Second Foreign Language (I)

Year 2 — Core

  • Survey of English Literature
  • Survey of American Literature
  • English Composition
  • Introduction to Linguistics

Years 3 & 4 — Electives

  • Shakespeare
  • The Novel · Drama · Poetry
  • Literary Criticism & Theory
  • Phonetics · Syntax · Film · Creative Writing

Students complete a two-year sequence in a second foreign language (French, German, Spanish, Japanese, or Latin) and choose electives across the department's literature, linguistics, and film tracks.

Where it leads

Foreign Languages and Literatures graduates are exceptional communicators. Our alumni work in:

  • Publishing & Editing
  • Teaching & Academia
  • Journalism & Media
  • Marketing & Communications
  • Law & Public Policy
  • Content & UX Writing

Admissions

Entry requirements

  • Strong results on the General Scholastic Ability Test (學測 / GSAT), with emphasis on English and Chinese
  • High School English Listening Comprehension Test result
  • A demonstrated interest in language, literature, and cross-cultural studies

Admission pathways

  1. Multi-Star Recommendation (繁星推薦)
  2. Personal Application (個人申請) — GSAT screening, then second-stage document review and interview
  3. Exam Placement (分發入學) via the Advanced Subjects Test (分科測驗)
  4. International & Special Selection (國際學生 / 特殊選才)
