About the programme

The B.A. in Foreign Languages and Literatures gives you a deep command of the English language and its literatures, from Chaucer to contemporary global fiction. You will learn to read critically, write persuasively, and think independently — skills valued in every profession.

Literature

Study poetry, drama, and the novel across periods, cultures, and continents.

Language & Linguistics

Explore how English works — its grammar, history, and role in the world today.

Creative Writing

Develop your own voice in fiction, poetry, and non-fiction workshops.

Curriculum

A structured path from foundations to specialism.

Year 1 — Foundations

  • Introduction to Literary Studies
  • The English Language: Structure & Sound
  • Reading Poetry
  • Academic Writing & Research Skills

Year 2 — Breadth

  • Shakespeare & Early Modern Drama
  • The Nineteenth-Century Novel
  • Sociolinguistics
  • Creative Writing Workshop

Year 3 — Specialism

  • Postcolonial & World Literatures
  • Contemporary Fiction
  • Dissertation Project
  • Optional: Editing & Publishing

Admissions

Entry requirements

  • A strong secondary school record in English or Humanities
  • English language proficiency (IELTS 6.5 or equivalent)
  • A short personal statement

How to apply

  1. Complete the online application form
  2. Upload transcripts and your personal statement
  3. Attend an optional interview
  4. Receive your decision within four weeks
